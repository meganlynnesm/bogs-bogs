Abstract

This project explores data, AI and the divide between urban and rural, global and local, connected and left behind.

Intertwined; a study in regeneration of landscapes - how the demand in technology and past degraded peatlands are both extractive industries, and how we might design a future where they are symbiotic rather than extractive.

Connected; a question of obsolescence: what in all of this is genuinely future, and what is merely the mysticism that accrues around AI and the technologies we imagine will save or reshape us. It draws on a historical echo between extractive industries; a shift from harvesting land to harvesting data which leads me to ideas of symbiosis. All leading to asking what remnants of data or technology actually persist across the life-cycle of this infrastructure.

Keywords: disparity, servers, regeneration, symbiotic, obsolescence.
